## 🎯 PRODUCT-MARKET FIT OPTIMIZATION

### **PMF Measurement Framework:**

#### **Sean Ellis Test Implementation:**
```
SURVEY METHODOLOGY:
- Target: Active Users (>30 days)
- Sample Size: 100+ responses
- Question: "How would you feel if you could no longer use this product?"
- Options: Very disappointed, Somewhat disappointed, Not disappointed
- Benchmark: >40% "Very disappointed" = Strong PMF

SEGMENTATION ANALYSIS:
- By User Persona
- By Use Case
- By Company Size
- By Industry Vertical
- By Geographic Region

IMPROVEMENT ACTIONS:
- <40%: Focus on Core Value Prop
- 40-50%: Optimize User Experience
- 50-60%: Scale & Growth Focus
- >60%: Market Leadership Strategy
```

#### **PMF Metrics Dashboard:**
